— .description (400 weight, clamp 1.2rem→1.8rem)

Project Overview

Hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs) are the foundation of the blood system, capable of both self-renewal and differentiation into all blood cell lineages. Our project focuses on engineering HSCs to correct genetic defects at the source.

Experimental Approach

We employ CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing combined with AAV6-mediated homology-directed repair to achieve precise genetic modifications in primary human CD34+ hematopoietic stem and progenitor cells, followed by functional validation in xenotransplantation models.

In the field of synthetic biology, the design of genetic circuits often involves iterative cycles of modeling, assembly, and testing. The choice of a typeface might seem trivial compared to the complexity of gene regulation, yet it plays a crucial role in communicating your results effectively. Judges and visitors to your iGEM wiki will spend considerable time reading your project description, experiments, and conclusions. Therefore, selecting a highly legible font ensures that your hard work is presented in the most accessible manner possible, reducing cognitive load and enhancing overall comprehension.
