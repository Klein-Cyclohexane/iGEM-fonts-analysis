Paragraph

In the field of synthetic biology, the design of genetic circuits often involves iterative cycles of modeling, assembly, and testing. The choice of a typeface might seem trivial compared to the complexity of gene regulation, yet it plays a crucial role in communicating your results effectively. Judges and visitors to your iGEM wiki will spend considerable time reading your project description, experiments, and conclusions. Therefore, selecting a highly legible font ensures that your hard work is presented in the most accessible manner possible, reducing cognitive load and enhancing overall comprehension.

Font Pairing in Context
How Abril Fatface and Figtree work together in the wiki's UI components.
1. Quorum Sensing Mechanism
Bacteria communicate via diffusible signal molecules (autoinducers) to coordinate population-level behaviors. When signal concentration reaches a threshold, it triggers synchronized gene expression — a process called quorum sensing. Our project targets this communication channel.
2. Biofilm Disruption Strategy
By enzymatically degrading specific autoinducers, we prevent biofilm formation without directly killing bacteria. This reduces selective pressure for resistance — a critical advantage over conventional antibiotic approaches in clinical and industrial settings.
Timeline Entry
Phase 1: Library Construction
We generated a diverse library of quorum-quenching enzyme variants using error-prone PCR and site-directed mutagenesis. Over 10,000 variants were screened for enhanced activity against Pseudomonas aeruginosa autoinducers.
